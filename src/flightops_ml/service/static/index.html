Statistical Integrity

The 81% Majority Class Accuracy Fallacy

Roughly 77% to 81% of commercial departures operate on time (DEP_DEL15 = 0). A trivial baseline predicting every flight on time achieves ~81% accuracy while delivering zero operational utility. Operational decision-making requires optimizing Precision-Recall AUC (PR-AUC) and strictly calibrated probability distributions.

Threshold Optimization: Empirical Validation τ* = 0.21 & Operating Cost Context

The operational threshold τ* = 0.21 was determined empirically by maximizing F1 score on the validation split (Sep–Oct 2025). In airline ground operations, decision errors have asymmetric impact: an unanticipated delay carries an estimated operational cost of ~$4,500 (missed turnaround slots, pilot duty limits, cascade disruptions), whereas a proactive turnaround buffer cushion costs ~$300. Under the classical theoretical cost formula τcost = 300 / (300 + 4500) ≈ 0.0625, the system would flag >65% of all flights, triggering severe dispatcher alarm fatigue. The empirical F1-optimal threshold τ* = 0.21 was selected as a defensible operating compromise, capturing 61.8% of test delays with a 36.6% alert precision.
